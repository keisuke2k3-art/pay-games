Parry just before the shot lands and your opponent is open for one clean hit. Parry a little early and you only deflect the shot. Draw and cancel, and you can bait a parry.

回避は自動で、1本につき2回まで。3発目は当たります。何も起きなければ間合いが詰まり、近いほど構えは速くなる。近間では見てからでは間に合いません。読むしかない。You dodge automatically, twice per point. The third hit lands. If nobody moves, the two of you close in, and the closer you get the faster a draw becomes. At close range you can't react any more. You have to read.

三つの手Three movesKATA

  • J構えるDraw

    構えてから撃つまでの間がある。もう一度押せば取り消し。There's a beat between drawing and firing. Press again to cancel.

  • F払うParry

    撃たれる直前なら見切り。早すぎると受け流すだけで、払った後には隙ができる。Right before the shot, it's a clean counter. Too early and you only deflect, and you're open afterwards.

  • J → J誘いBait

    構えてすぐ取り消して、相手の払いを空振りさせる。ただし6秒構えずに待つと「圧」で回避が1つ減る。Draw and cancel to make them parry the air. But wait six seconds without drawing and the pressure costs you a dodge.
